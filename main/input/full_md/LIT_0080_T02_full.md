# Retraction notice to “Clinical Observation and Evaluation of Health Management Intervention in Controlling Senile Chronic Diseases such as Hyperlipidemia” [SLAS Technology 33 (2025) 100318]

Hongxia Liu <sup>a,b,\*</sup>

<sup>a</sup> School of Pharmacy, Jiangsu Medical College, Yancheng 224005, Jiangsu, China

<sup>b</sup> School of Pharmacy, Faculty of Health and Medical Sciences, Taylor’s University, 47500, Subang Jaya, Selangor, Malaysia

This article has been retracted: please see Elsevier policy on article withdrawal (https://www.elsevier.com/about/policies-and-standards /article-withdrawal). Post-publication an investigation conducted by Elsevier’s Research Integrity & Publishing Ethics team on behalf of the journal has found that ethical approval was not obtained for the research presented in this publication. There are concerns that the author appears

to have used a Generative AI source in the writing process of the paper without disclosure. This breaches the journal’s policy on ethical approvals and usage of generative AI. Consequently, the Editor has lost confidence in the integrity of the article and has determined it should be retracted.
