## Abstract

Purpose – Given the increasing attention on ChatGPT in academia due to its advanced features and capabilities, this study aims to examine the links among Artificial intelligence (AI), knowledge sharing, ethics, academia and libraries in educational institutions. Moreover, this study also aims to provide a literature base while discussing recent trends in AI and ChatGPT technologies, highlighting their specific uses in institutions. Design/methodology/approach – The paper involves a structured interview format where a human interviewer poses questions “Qs” in ChatGPT, related to knowledge sharing, ethics, academia and libraries. Moreover a literature base is also provide to discussed recent trends in AI and ChatGPT technologies, highlighting their specific uses in institutions. Findings – The study find out that AI and ChatGPT technologies in educational institutions affect knowledge sharing, ethical consideration, academia and libraries. This study also highlights literature directions for the trends and proper use of the AI and ChatGPT among institutions, such as improving student-learning engagement. Originality/value – This research contributes to the prior literature by offering an in-depth review of current uses and applications of AI and ChatGPT in educational institutions. It not only highlights key trends and innovations but also provides insights and guidelines for future research. This study also provides insights and guidelines for future research. Furthermore, the article emphasizes the potential impact of AI and ChatGPT on the future of education and technology.

Keywords Artificial intelligence, ChatGPT, Knowledge sharing, Ethics, Academia, Libraries, Institutions Paper type Research paper

Furthermore, while GPT technology is proficient at generating text, it lacks full comprehension and understanding of the context and significance of the generated content.

## 5. Discussion

The introductory section of this study briefly presents a comprehensive overview of ChatGPT, and AI-based chatboat has garnered significant attention in the education sector due to its potential to enhance competitiveness and support educators. The initial part of the manuscript is dedicated to exploring the inception, functionalities and framework of GPT, and the foundational algorithm upon which ChatGPT is built. The findings revealed that ChatGPT3 has the capability to enhance the efficacy and precision of information dissemination. It can be utilized, for instance, to develop chatbots and virtual assistance to provide information on a variety of subjects and responding to frequently asked inquiries. This can be especially beneficial for academic institutions and libraries that must provide information access to a large number of individuals. The finding is consistent with previous studies that ChatGPT have a prominent role in knowledge sharing, collaboration and learning in the digital workplace context (Fahad et al., 2024; Sumbal and Amber, 2024). The findings also concluded that ethical considerations must be taken into account, including privacy and bias. The results is tie with the findings of Dave et al. (2023) and Stahl and Eke (2024). They stated that the potential use of ChatGPTand other AI tools must poses ethical and legal concerns. These include possible copyright laws, legal complications and other needed transparency in AI-generated content. Furthermore, ethical ramifications of utilizing AI language-based models for knowledge sharing, such as ChatGPT3, must be taken into account for proper usage in academia. Such models can run the risks of perpetuating discrimination and biases that are inherent in the data on which they are trained. Consequently, it is critical to monitor and assess the output of these models with extreme care to ensure that they do not propagate false information or detrimental stereotypes.

The results of the current study further revealed that academics might utilize ChatGPT3 for writing and research assistance. For instance, it can help researchers locate pertinent literature on a given subject or generate summaries of scholarly articles. The statement is further supported by Bin-Nashwan et al. (2023), it is critical to guarantee any utilization of ChatGPT3 in scholarly investigations is unambiguous and conspicuously cited in any resultant publications. However, it is essential to avoid using exact information from articles due to similarity issues, and the references have not usually properly generated. The finding of the current study lastly concluded that ChatGPT3 could be utilized in libraries to assist users in locating pertinent books and other resources and delivering support for research inquiries. It is ties with the results of Adetayo (2023) and Lund et al. (2024). They endorsed that it is critical to ensure that any library implementation of ChatGPT3 is conducted in an ethical and transparent manner, with due consideration given to any potential biases in the training data. While highlighting the potential benefits of ChatGPT for librarianship and academic research, this article also issues admonitions regarding potential drawbacks. Hence, in order to advance our research and education, it is vital that we determine how to collaborate with this technology in a responsible and ethical manner.

## 5.1 Implications

The study underscores the advantages AI and ChatGPT to facilitate better knowledge sharing among students and faculty. ChatGPT enables better knowledge sharing. In addition, it is acting as a virtual assistant to help manage tasks, schedules and other activities. ChatGPT enables rapid and accessible knowledge sharing by providing immediate responses to queries across a wide range of topics (Nazir and Wang, 2023). This can democratize access to information, allowing people from various backgrounds to benefit from expert-level knowledge on various topics. Moreover, this study underscores the importance of addressing ethical concerns. ChatGPT raises key ethical considerations regarding the usage of AI in generating content (Uzun, 2023). It highlights the need for responsible AI development, ensuring transparency, addressing biases and encouraging ethical guidelines for AI usage. It further stresses the safety of copyrights in AI-generated content. ChatGPT can serve as a valuable tool for research and development, assisting scholars in literature reviews, hypotheses development, conceptualizing complex concepts and providing a route map for study conduction (Atkinson, 2024). This study also promotes the usage of ChatGPT and prompts considerations about academic integrity, plagiarism and the proper citation of AIgenerated content. The emergence of ChatGPT among educational institutions can help students in clarify the basic concepts and technical tasks (Javaid et al., 2023). It also helps students save time and perceive the topic in a more accurate way, reducing conflicts of opinion and facilitating better understanding. Lastly, ChatGPT can improve library services by providing personalized recommendations, answering user inquiries and facilitating virtual reference services.

## 5.2 Conclusion

Academic institutions realize the benefits of adopting a digital-first approach, the implementation of sophisticated technologies like chatbots and virtual reality is expanding in prevalence. Concerns do, however, exist regarding the implementation of AI in academic institutions, specifically privacy infringement and unequal access to digital resources and technology. Academic establishments are tasked with striking a delicate equilibrium between infusing students’ lives with institution-wide significance and safeguarding their privacy. Online monitoring platforms may also exacerbate socioeconomic disparities by negatively impacting students who lack access to an ideal, peaceful working environment. The COVID-19 pandemic has brought to light the digital divide within educational institutions, as some students are deprived of technology and Internet access. This discrepancy has led to a divergence in educational opportunities and digital literacy. It is of the utmost importance to ensure that the implementation of cutting-edge technologies in educational environments, such as ChatGPT, is conducted in an ethical and transparent manner.
