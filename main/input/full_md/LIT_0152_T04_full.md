# What Factors Afect the Adoption Intention and Actual Use of ChatGPT in Higher Education? The Moderating Role of Academic Integrity

Faiz Subhani<sup>1</sup> · Subayal Aftab Khan<sup>2</sup> · Maqsood Ahmad Sandhu<sup>2</sup> · Muhammad Farrukh Shahzad<sup>3</sup>

Accepted: 23 May 2025 / Published online: 9 June 2025

© Association for Educational Communications & Technology 2025

## Abstract

The adoption of generative AI in education has revolutionized science by producing human-like texts through contextual understanding, as OpenAI ChatGPT shows. OpenAI's widely used large language model, such as ChatGPT, has become quite popular, especially in higher education. This study examines diferent factors influencing the adoption and actual use of ChatGPT to improve student learning outcomes. The supporting theory (UTAUT2) is applied to back our conceptual framework. This study applies the PLS-SEM method for inspecting collected data from 361 Pakistani university students. The findings reveal that performance expectancy, efort expectancy, ChatGPT self-eficacy, social influence, hedonic motivation, and price value significantly influence ChatGPT adoption intention. Conversely, habit and facilitation conditions negatively influence ChatGPT adoption intention. Furthermore, results show that ChatGPT adoption intention significantly influences the actual use of ChatGPT. Additionally, academic integrity is a significant moderator between adoption intention and influences actual use of ChatGPT. The study's findings ofer valuable implications for educators and policymakers, emphasizing the need to enhance students' use of ChatGPT. By maintaining academic integrity, institutes can support responsible adoption, ensuring ChatGPT's efective use to improve learning outcomes in higher education.

Keywords Actual use of ChatGPT · Adoption intention · Hedonic motivation · Academic integrity · Higher education · UTAUT2

## Introduction

The use of ChatGPT (Generative Pre-trained Transformer), developed by OpenAI, to integrate artificial intelligence (AI) into education has significantly changed the academic landscape (Tlili et al., 2023). The development of AI has made significant progress, particularly in the area of language generation, from its introduction at the 1956 Dartmouth conference to modern transformer-based models such as ChatGPT (Shahzad et al., 2024a). Higher education can benefit greatly from using ChatGPT, but there are also disadvantages, including the spread of misleading information and concerns about data security and privacy. In addition, as a research tool, ChatGPT accelerates the research process by creating preliminary research paper drafts, summarizing scientific texts, and facilitating the retrieval of materials (Maheshwari, 2023).

However, it is necessary to recognize the ethical issues and limitations associated with using ChatGPT in education (Kamalov et al., 2023). Educational institutions must consider factors such as academic integrity and the promotion of critical thinking related to the implementation of this educational technology (Bin-Nashwan et al., 2023). One of the major problems is that over-reliance on ChatGPT and other AI-driven tools can impair students'critical thinking skills and intrinsic motivation, which could impact their success in the classroom and real-world situations (Maheshwari, 2023).

Along with drawbacks, ChatGPT has the potential to renovate education by enabling customized learning experiences, assisting with administrative tasks, supporting research, and developing interactive learning environments (Shahzad, Xu, An, Zahid, et al., 2025). ChatGPT's best implementation and adoption in higher education depends on some factors, including performance expectancy (PE), efort expectancy (EE), ChatGPT self-eficacy (CGSE), social influence (SI), hedonic motivation (HM), price value (PV), habit (HAB) and facilitation conditions (FC).

A prior study expressed performance expectancy as the perceived usefulness of ChatGPT in achieving desired outcomes (Ma & Huo, 2023). Similarly, the term efort expectancy, which relates to the ease of using the system, is pivotal in determining whether students employ ChatGPT in their academic journey (Bouteraa et al., 2024). Moving forward, ChatGPT self-eficacy is the confidence in one's ability to efectively utilize ChatGPT, a crucial psychological construct shaping an individual's willingness to engage with generative AI technology (Gao et al., 2024). Socially influenced students will likely adopt ChatGPT if their peers use it successfully. Positive testimonials and shared experiences can drive wider acceptance and use (Baek & Kim, 2023). Similarly, individuals who integrate ChatGPT into their learning and openly endorse its benefits can influence adoption. Hedonic motivation is another factor in students that creates pleasure and enjoyment derived from technology such as ChatGPT (Strzelecki, 2023). It can be a potent driver of adoption intention, as it taps into the emotional aspect of technology usage (Jo, 2023b).

The price value, which encompasses considerations of cost and benefits, may either facilitate or hinder adoption among budget-conscious students (Strzelecki, 2023). Moreover, the formation of habit, a behavioral pattern of regularly utilizing ChatGPT, can significantly impact its long-term integration into the daily academic routines of students. This factor is particularly relevant as students establish familiarity and comfort with the technology over time (Jan et al., 2023). Facilitation conditions refer to the support provided to users for utilizing ChatGPT efectively. Reliable internet access, availability of devices, and user-friendly interfaces are essential (Menon & Shilpa, 2023). Universities that provide clear guidelines on using ChatGPT appropriately can encourage its use by providing a framework within which students and educators can operate confidently (Qu & Wu, 2024). Academic integrity, ensuring that ChatGPT is used ethically and transparently, is crucial in education (Bin-Nashwan et al., 2023). This might involve implementing plagiarism detection software and emphasizing the importance of original thought and proper attribution (Cotton et al., 2024).

While previous studies have explored the adoption of technology in education, limited research has investigated the factors influencing ChatGPT adoption and its actual use among university students, particularly in the context of academic integrity. Existing literature lacks insight into the moderating role of academic integrity in shaping students’ responsible use of ChatGPT. Moreover, there is a scarcity of studies applying the UTAUT2 model to understand ChatGPT adoption in higher education (Shahzad et al., 2024b; Strzelecki, 2023). This study addresses these gaps by examining key factors influencing ChatGPT adoption and exploring how academic integrity impacts its use for enhancing learning outcomes. Filling these research gaps would improve the usage of ChatGPT in Pakistani higher education. Therefore, this study considers factors such as PE, EE, SI, CGSE, HM, PV, HAB and FC on ADI and AU of ChatGPT in university students. Furthermore, academic integrity is a significant moderator between ADI and AU of ChatGPT.

## Theoretical Underpinning and Hypotheses Development

## UTAUT2 Theory

The Unified Theory of Acceptance and Use of Technology 2 (UTAUT2), originally formulated by researcher Venkatesh was developed in 2003 Venkatesh et al. (2003) and further refined in 2012 Venkatesh et al. (2012), represented a widely accepted framework that explains technology adoption in various contexts, including ChatGPT integration. UTAUT2 includes crucial elements such as PE, EE, SI, HM, PV, HAB and FC collectively illuminate individuals'intentions and behaviors related to technology adoption. This study used components such as (PE, EE, SI, HM, PV, HAB and FC of UTAUT2 theory to support the study model. Performance expectancy refers to individuals'perceptions of how incorporating new technology, such as ChatGPT, can increase productivity or performance on specific tasks, particularly in educational environments (Menon & Shilpa, 2023). Efort expectancy, or users'judgments of technology's simplicity and ease of use, has a major impact on students'adoption intentions when evaluating the user-friendliness of ChatGPT (Strzelecki, 2023). The social influence construct looks at how much people think significant others—friends, family, and coworkers, for example—think they should use Chat-GPT. Positive recommendations, social norms, and colleague endorsements can significantly impact a user’s adoption of ChatGPT (Maheshwari, 2023).

Hedonic motivation measures an individual's perception of the enjoyment or entertainment of using technology and potentially influences students'behavior when adopting ChatGPT (Jan et al., 2023). Price value assesses the perceived costs and benefits of implementing technology and impacts students'adoption decisions when the pros and cons of ChatGPT are considered (Jo, 2023b). Habit refers to how previous experiences and regular technology use influence current adoption and potentially influence students'decisions based on previous interactions with AI technologies or their habitual use of ChatGPT (Strzelecki, 2024). Facilitation conditions are the resources and support available to users that facilitate the use of ChatGPT. Access to necessary technology (e.g., internet connectivity), technical support, and user guides can enhance the likelihood of adoption and continued use (Menon & Shilpa, 2023). Applying UTAUT2, it becomes evident that the above factors contribute to the adoption and use of ChatGPT. Therefore, this study used the UTAUT2 theory to support the framework of ChatGPT adoption intention. Eventually, using the UTAUT2 multidimensional theory, researchers can better understand the factors that impact the acceptance of technology like ChatGPT in higher education.

## Performance expectancy and ChatGPT adoption intention

Understanding the elements influencing users'willingness to accept such innovations in integrating cutting-edge technological tools such as ChatGPT into educational environments is ongoing (Ma & Huo, 2023). Performance expectancy is at the heart of technology adoption models, a powerful indicator of consumers'propensity to adopt new technologies (Strzelecki & ElArabawy, 2024). A past study stated that performance expectancy is the expectation of consumers to achieve better performance and results from ChatGPT technology (Strzelecki, 2023). This includes students'opinions on how, in the ChatGPT context, an AI-driven tool could improve learning outcomes, academic performance, and overall classroom success. In addition, automating tasks such as content creation, quiz preparation, and grading increases performance standards by making the tool easier to use and saving time (Bouteraa et al., 2024). Individuals with higher technological expertise will likely achieve superior outcomes than those less acquainted with AI methods. While performance expectations significantly impact the adoption of ChatGPT, factors such as institutional backing, personal preferences, usability, and sociocultural influences also wield considerable significance (Duong et al., 2023).

Finally, students'desire to use ChatGPT in study is largely shaped by how they expect it to enhance their performance. Considering this discussion, the following hypothesis is put forth:

H1: Performance expectancy positively impacts Chat-GPT adoption intention.

## Efort Expectancy and ChatGPT Adoption Intention

Students have high expectations for efort and are likelier to employ ChatGPT in their education (Strzelecki & ElArabawy, 2024). ChatGPT adoption in educational contexts is expected to expand due to factors such as easy usability, time-saving advantages, resource accessibility, supporting training, and positive user experiences (Wang et al., 2022). Students are more at ease integrating communication technology tools and platforms into their lesson plans because of their user-friendly interfaces, clear instructions, and low need for technical knowledge. It is critical to consider the impact of work expectations on people's willingness to adopt and use technology, especially when it comes to students using ChatGPT (Shahzad et al., 2024b ). Efort expectancy is a crucial element of the UTAUT2, which is the perception of how easy it will be to adopt a particular technology. It includes elements such as perceived complexity, ease of use, and simplicity (Ma & Huo, 2023). About ChatGPT, efort expectancy describes how students perceive the usability and simplicity of the platform during the learning phase. Use of ChatGPT to improve research, provide targeted support, and improve learning environments (Strzelecki, 2023). Similarly, expectations of the efort involved in adopting Chat-GPT among university students are influenced by several factors, such as the usefulness, usability, and accessibility of the platform, which greatly influence students'opinions. This illustrates how crucial the efort expectations are for using ChatGPT in higher education (Strzelecki & ElArabawy, 2024). The following hypothesis is put out in light of above discussion:

H2: Efort expectancy positively impacts ChatGPT adoption intention.

## ChatGPT Self‑eficacy and ChatGPT Adoption Intention

Self-eficacy is the belief that one can perform a certain task proficiently. Integrating generative AI technologies such as ChatGPT into educational environments has largely replaced the concept of self-eficacy (Shahzad et al., 2024a). Selfeficacy is a crucial factor in determining the propensity of university students to adopt and use ChatGPT, an AI-driven platform, productively. Self-eficacy is a student's confidence in their ability to use, understand, and benefit from the Chat-GPT application (Shahzad et al., 2024c). Multiple studies have shown a substantial relationship between college students'willingness to use ChatGPT and their trust in this artificial intelligence application (Shahzad et al., 2024a; Strzelecki & ElArabawy, 2024). Students who are confident in their abilities are more likely to use ChatGPT for academic purposes. Our study arguments are consistent with the social cognitive theory proposed by Bandura, which postulates that self-eficacy is necessary for performing achievable actions (Bandura, 1977). Students'self-eficacy increases when they are properly trained, coached, and familiarized with the features of ChatGPT. Their sense of self-eficacy greatly influences the willingness of university students to accept and use ChatGPT technology. Successfully incorporating selfeficacy into higher education must be promoted through instruction, support, and positive perspectives regarding its value (Shahzad et al., 2024a).

Taking these ideas into account, the study's hypothesis is as follows:

H3: ChatGPT self-eficacy positively impacts ChatGPT adoption intention.

## Social Influence and ChatGPT Adoption Intention

Social influence refers to how others shape individuals'thoughts, feelings, and behaviors. Through social influence, individuals accept information from others as evidence of reality (Shahzad, Xu, An, Asif, et al., 2025). Individuals who observe their peers using and endorsing ChatGPT may feel compelled to adopt it themselves. This is especially true in environments where peer opinion is highly valued, such as workplaces or academic settings (Strzelecki & ElArabawy, 2024). Students often rely on the experiences and recommendations of others to make informed decisions. When trusted sources or influencers in the tech community endorse ChatGPT, it can significantly boost confidence in the tool and increase adoption rates (Maheshwari, 2023). Personal recommendations from friends, colleagues, and family members can be highly persuasive. Hearing positive experiences and success stories about ChatGPT can motivate others to try it. Positive discussions, shared experiences, and endorsements by influencers can create a favorable environment for adoption (Menon & Shilpa, 2023). Social influence plays a critical role in the adoption intention of ChatGPT by leveraging peer pressure, social proof, normative influence, and informational influence. By understanding and leveraging these social dynamics, promoters of ChatGPT can efectively increase their user base and ensure its successful integration into various professional and personal settings (Ma & Huo, 2023). So, it is hypothesized that:

H4: ChatGPT social influence positively impacts Chat-GPT adoption intention.

## Hedonic Motivation and ChatGPT Adoption Intention

The focus on enhancing learning experiences has sparked considerable interest in incorporating AI models like ChatGPT in education sector (Lai et  al., 2023). When evaluating educational institutions'adoption of this technology, it is important to consider the elements influencing students'willingness to use ChatGPT. In particular, hedonic motivation is important in shaping people's willingness to adopt new technologies (Al-Azawei & Alowayr, 2020). To explore the connection between hedonic motivation and the inclination of university students to utilize ChatGPT, particularly focusing on its implications for technology use in education. Hedonic motivation refers to the pleasure or satisfaction of using a specific technology (Menon & Shilpa, 2023). In the context of educational technology, it refers to the feeling of achievement, joy, or pleasure that comes from using technologies such as ChatGPT (Strzelecki, 2023). This emotional component significantly influences attitudes and intentions towards adopting new technologies. Furthermore, students'hedonic motivation is enhanced when they use ChatGPT in academia, such as when creating the course content and research support (Kim et al., 2023). ChatGPT's diverse learning styles enhance students'educational performance. Understanding hedonic motivation influences educational technology as ChatGPT emphasizes user experience for engaging, enjoyable learning, balancing utility, pleasure, and ethics in integration for success (Shahzad et al., 2024d). Hence, from the above arguments, it is derived that hedonic incentives influence university students'intention to use ChatGPT. It is hypothesized that:

H5: Hedonic motivation positively impacts ChatGPT adoption intention.

## Price Value and ChatGPT Adoption Intention

The pr ice value is impor tant in deter mining users'intentions to use ChatGPT, a generative AI tool (Jo, 2023b). It impacts the adoption in both professional and educational settings. The price value of ChatGPT is pivotal in users'adoption decisions, correlating with its utility against the associated cost (Jo, 2024). The numerous applications of OpenAI's ChatGPT in communication, education, research, and content creation have attracted attention. Some emphasize ChatGPT's perceived benefits like improved learning, instant feedback, task automation, research aid, and meeting user needs (Shahzad, Xu, An, Zahid, et al., 2025). Clear communication about features, costs, and benefits can lead to a deeper thought of the value proposition of ChatGPT usage. For ChatGPT to seamlessly integrate across multiple domains, it is important to consider these complicated aspects of pricing value (Strzelecki, 2023). This perception can stem from several factors, such as the availability of free or cheaper alternatives, the frequency of use, and the relevance of the service to their academic and personal needs (Shahzad, Xu, An, Asif, et al., 2025). University students typically have limited financial resources, as they rely on allowances, parttime jobs, or student loans. When evaluating a service like ChatGPT, they must consider whether the cost fits within their budget (Jo, 2024). High subscription fees or upfront costs can make the service less attractive, as students may prefer to allocate their funds to essential expenses like tuition, textbooks, and living costs (Rahman et al., 2023). Hence, from the above discussion, it is concluded that:

H6: Price value negatively impacts ChatGPT adoption intention.

## Habit and ChatGPT Adoption Intention

The habituation process influences users'intentions to adopt and use ChatGPT, an advanced generative AI for academic purposes. The consistent and frequent use of ChatGPT in higher education can be attributed to established practices, usability features, and routines (Strzelecki, 2023). In technology adoption, habit formation is about integrating a particular tool into daily routines to establish a consistent usage pattern. This includes unconscious and automatic interactions with technology as individuals engage without conscious consideration or choice (Biloš & Budimir, 2024). A past researcher argued that the habit loop, comprising three components: trigger, routine, and reward, ofers a valuable framework for comprehending the development of habits in the context of technology adoption (Strzelecki, 2024). Daily use of ChatGPT leads to rewards (technological advantages) and ultimately represents habitual behavior. Similarly, students adopt habits and discover a positive relationship between habit development and continued use of ChatGPT (Strzelecki, 2023). Among educators and university students, several factors influence habit formation regarding ChatGPT use, including perceived ease of use, interaction frequency, task compatibility, perceived benefits, social influence, institutional support, and their impact on both intention and actual use of technology (Shahzad et al., 2024b ). In light of these findings, the following hypothesis is presented:

H7: Habit positively impacts ChatGPT adoption intention.

## Facilitation Condition and ChatGPT Adoption Intention

Facilitation conditions are external factors that make it easier or harder for users to adopt and use technology. Students may have consistent access to laptops, tablets, or smartphones capable of running ChatGPT efectively (Ma & Huo, 2023). Many students lack these devices, and their ability to use ChatGPT is compromised. Reliable and high-speed internet access is crucial for using ChatGPT. Students in areas with poor connectivity or those who cannot aford stable internet services face significant barriers (Menon & Shilpa, 2023). The absence of adequate technical support for troubleshooting and guidance can deter students from using ChatGPT. Students encounter issues, and no help is available; they are less likely to adopt the technology (Elshaer et al., 2024). Universities do not endorse or integrate Chat-GPT into their systems and curricula, and students might see less value in using it. Institutional support in the form of oficial recommendations, integration into coursework, or provision of accounts can encourage use (Qu & Wu, 2024). Concerns about how personal data and usage data are handled can deter students. Students who are unsure about ChatGPT's privacy policies may be reluctant to use it. Students are unaware of ChatGPT's potential benefits and are unlikely to adopt it. Misconceptions or negative publicity about ChatGPT can deter students from trying it out. Fears about security breaches or misuse of information can also negatively impact adoption (Elshaer et al., 2024). It is hypothesized that:

H8: Facilitation conditions negatively impact ChatGPT adoption intention.

## Adoption Intention and Actual Use of ChatGPT

Adoption intentions afect the actual use of these technologies, and they have become very popular due to their introduction into educational institutions. In particular, a wellknown example in this area that has attracted attention in the education sector is ChatGPT, established by OpenAI (Sohail et al., 2023). Adoption intention is a measure of a person's willingness to adopt a technology that has a major influence on how the technology is used (Davis, 1989). This goal encourages students to use ChatGPT in learning environments (Shahzad et al., 2024b). Consequently, students attracted to ChatGPT due to its obvious advantages and ease of use tend to integrate it more into their academic activities. Notably, students'positive opinion of ChatGPT's value in supporting learning or streamlining tasks is positively related to active use of the platform. In addition, positive adoption intention promotes further integration by creating a welcoming climate in educational institutions (Tlili et al.,

2023). However, this correlation can be modified by contextual elements such as sociocultural efects, institutional support, and technical competence (Maheshwari, 2023). Students'adoption intentions and future usage correlate significantly with their understanding of AI, institutional backing, and societal views. Adoption goals heavily impact ChatGPT's classroom usage. Some, contextual dificulties demand deep comprehension for their efective integration into teaching methods (Saif et al., 2023). From the above arguments, it is hypothesized that:

H9: Adoption intention positively impacts the actual use of ChatGPT.

## Moderating Role of Academic Integrity

Academic integrity encompasses the principles of honesty, fairness, trust, and responsibility in academic work. Students with a strong sense of academic integrity may scrutinize the legitimacy and acceptability of using ChatGPT (Bin-Nashwan et al., 2023). They are more likely to use the tool ethically, ensuring it aligns with institutional guidelines and personal moral standards. This means they might use Chat-GPT primarily for idea generation, clarifying concepts, or as a supplementary tool rather than relying on it for completing assignments or exams (Cotton et al., 2024). These students are likely to question the reliability and truth of information generated by ChatGPT. They may cross-verify the outputs with credible sources, leading to a more cautious and responsible tool use. Students who value academic integrity are more likely to adhere to institutional policies and guidelines regarding the use of AI tools (Kamoun, 2024). The institution has clear policies on how ChatGPT should be used, and these students will follow them, which may moderate their actual use of the tool despite high adoption intentions. Students with strong academic integrity are more self-regulated and hold themselves accountable for their learning. They use ChatGPT to complement their knowledge and skills rather than replace their efort (Lai et al., 2024). This self-regulation ensures that their use of ChatGPT aligns with ethical standards. The actual use of ChatGPT by students with high academic integrity tends to enhance their learning outcomes as they use the tool to support and deepen their understanding (Bin-Nashwan et al., 2023). Students with a high sense of academic integrity are likely to use ChatGPT responsibly and ethically, aligning their actual use with their initial intentions. From the above discussion, it is hypothesized that:

H10: Academic integrity moderates the association between adoption intention and actual use of ChatGPT.

## Research Methodology

## Survey Development and Measures

The survey instrument utilized in this investigation was derived from the UTAUT2 models, with additional reference to the existing literature on ChatGPT acceptance and usage. The first part of the survey instrument represents respondents'demographic data, while the other part contains information about the variables adopted from prior literature (Shahzad et al., 2024a; Strzelecki, 2023). Chat-GPT adoption intention (ADI) and actual use of ChatGPT (AU) are the dependent variables taken into account in this study. Performance expectancy (PE), efort expectancy (EE), ChatGPT self-eficacy (CGSE), social influence (SI), hedonic motivation (HM), price value (PV), habit (HAB), and facilitation conditions (FC) are independent variables in this study. Academic integrity (ACI) is a moderator variable. Every item in the survey had a rating on a Likert scale of 1 to 5, where 1 represented strongly disagree and 5 represented strongly agree. Constructs such as ADI, AU, PE, EE, HM, HAB, CGSE and ACI were composed of a three-item scale, whereas the PV and SI had four-item scales. As shown in Table 2, the survey instrument items were taken from previous studies. All items of constructs are displayed in Table 2 in detail.

## Participants and Procedure

For the current study, data is collected from university students located in Lahore, Pakistan. The links to the survey were shared on open social media sites in an efort to attract respondents. The following screening questions are added to the description portion of the questionnaire to validate the current study's authenticity. 1) Are you currently enrolled in a higher education institution (Yes, No). 2) Have you ever used ChatGPT for academic purposes (Yes, No). The students familiar with and using the ChatGPT for their academic activities are only considered true participants of the study, while the rest of them are excluded. The target population for this study was university students at both private and public universities in Pakistan, who were selected using purposive sampling (Shaofeng Wang et al., 2023). Only students who had utilized ChatGPT as an educational tool were included in this study to ensure that the participants were suficiently familiar with the survey questions. The Higher Education Commission (HEC) of Pakistan's website was used as a source of data collection to determine the top rank six public and private universities, as past studies used (Ali et al., 2024; Malik et al., 2024). This ranking is based on research output, faculty qualifications, and student enrollment.

Furthermore, privacy and ethical considerations related to data collection, such as informed consent, anonymity, and data protection measures, should be explicitly addressed to ensure the study's ethical rigour. The researchers circulated 470 questionnaires and received 410 completed ones. After a rigorous data evaluation, which included correcting outliers and unengaged responses, 361 responses with a 76% response rate, the data remained accessible for analysis. According to Hair et al. (2017), a suitable sample size for this study would be calculated by multiplying the number of items by 10, resulting in a minimum of 350 participants (35 × 10 = 350). The final sample of the current study consists of 148 females (41%) and 213 males (59%) responses. Further demographic specifics are provided in Table 1.

## Analysis and Results

The ten proposed hypotheses were examined using structural equation modelling (SEM) analysis of the data obtained for this study. Because SEM combines multiple regression analysis and confirmatory factor analysis (CFA), so it was a suitable analysis method for examining causal links between variables (Henseler et al., 2016). As mentioned in the findings and analysis section, a number of experiments were run before the hypothesis testing using SEM. Using the statistical software Smart PLS 4.0, we have examined the suggested theoretical model of ChatGPT adoption and actual use. We determined that PLS-SEM was the best method for the following research to take into account the intricate model of moderation and mediation analysis (Hair et al., 2017; Hamdollah & Baghaei, 2016). The measurement mode and the structural model were evaluated in two stages of the study using the SmartPLS software.

## Common Method Bias

In order to minimize potential bias, the survey items were examined for the presence of common method bias (CMB) using the Harman single-factor analysis technique (Podsakof et al., 2003). After factors were eliminated, loadings were looked at, and it was discovered that none of the components explained the majority of the variation, proving the lack of bias in the data. Herman's single-factor analysis shows that a single factor accounts for less than 50% of the variance (Liu et al., 2024). Our analysis revealed that a single component accounted for the fluctuation, which is far less than the 50% CBM bias criterion. Furthermore, CMB is calculated using the variance inflation factor (VIF). This study demonstrates that the VIF value of each variable is less than 5. Thus, Herman's single-factor and VIF are satisfied, and the CMB with actual data is not a problem.

## Measurement Model

PLS-SEM is best suited for exploratory research that thoroughly assesses both the measurement and the structural model (Henseler et al., 2016). Regardless of sample size or data distribution, this method is flexible enough to handle simultaneous connection models and uses bootstrapping techniques and estimation methods, making it appropriate for new and exploratory studies (Hair et al., 2017). We examined the discriminant validity, convergent validity, and reliability of the measurement model. Cronbach's alpha (α), composite reliability (CR) and extracted average variance (AVE) were used to assess reliability. All of the constructs showed good construct reliability, with Cronbach's alpha values (0.700–0.992) and CR values (0.821–0.994) over the suggested criterion of 0.70 and AVE values (0.604–0.984) above the suggested threshold of 0.50 (Bagozzi & Yi, 1989). Convergent validity was evaluated by verifying the factor loading on each of their individual constructs (Shahzad et al., 2024a). All factor loadings (0.731–0.995) for the items in Table 2 were higher than 0.70, demonstrating adequate convergent validity.

When inter-construct correlations were less than the square root of the constructs'AVE, discriminant validity was demonstrated. The constructs have suficient discriminant validity, as Table 3 shows. Discriminant validity was verified by applying the criteria established by Fornell and Larcker. The discriminant validity analysis constitutes an evaluative process aimed at assessing the degree to which discrete constructs within a research framework manifest genuine distinctiveness from one another (Fornell & Larcker, 1981). As seen in Table 3, the square roots of AVE exceeded the associated correlations, satisfying the requirement for discriminant validity. The method used was the Heterotrait-Monotrait (HTMT) ratio, which is commonly used to evaluate discriminant validity (Hair et al., 2017). All of the HTMT values of this study fell below the suggested threshold of 0.90 (Bagozzi & Yi, 1989). As a result, discriminant validity was proved.

## Discussion and Conclusions

The study's primary goal was to use the UTAUT2 model to evaluate diferent factors influencing ChatGPT adoption and actual use in Pakistani universities. With the aid of UTAUT2 components, a conceptual framework was created to achieve this goal and evaluate ChatGPT adoption and its actual impact on the education sector of Pakistan. Collected data from 361 Pakistani university students who utilized OpenAI, such as ChatGPT, for academic purposes to validate the suggested conceptual model statistically. This study also examines how factors PE, EE, CGSE, SI, HM, PV, HAB, and FC afect the ChatGPT adoption and actual use in the education sector of Pakistan. Additional academic integrity significantly moderates the association between adoption and actual ChatGPT use. The study's findings are categorized into ten primary sections:

First, the study substantiates that performance expectancy (PE) significantly influences the adoption intention of ChatGPT. The findings corroborate prior research Strzelecki and ElArabawy (2024), highlighting the indispensable role of users'expectations regarding ChatGPT's performance in shaping their intentions to adopt this ChatGPT technology. Specifically, emphasizing the importance of performance expectations in shaping adoption intentions within the domain of ChatGPT enhances this premise (Duong et al., 2023).

Second, the investigation highlights that efort expectancy positively impacts ChatGPT adoption intention. This finding aligns with prior studies (Ma & Huo, 2023; Strzelecki, 2024) emphasizing the significance of efort expectancy in determining users'intentions to adopt ChatGPT technologies. Efort expectancy comprises elements such as ease of learning, and cognitive eforts required to utilize technology. Moreover, the strong constructive afiliation between efort expectancy (EE) and adoption intention highlights the importance of user perceptions and experiences in the design and implementation of ChatGPT (Ma & Huo, 2023).

Third, the investigation further reveals that ChatGPT self-eficacy positively impacts adoption intention. This finding aligns with assertions that an individual's trust in their capabilities to engage with and utilize new technologies efectively strongly influences their willingness to adopt them (Shahzad et al., 2024a). As per Bandura (1977), selfeficacy serves as a pivotal determinant in shaping behaviors and intentions, resonating with the current study's outcomes. These findings provide guidelines for students aiming to enhance ChatGPT adoption rates, emphasizing the pivotal role of self-eficacy beliefs in this process.

Fourth, social influence positively impacts ChatGPT adoption intention. According to a past study Maheshwari (2023), social influence suggests that when students perceive that their peers and educators value and use ChatGPT, they are more likely to consider adopting the technology themselves. Peer usage and academic endorsement create a conducive environment for technology adoption, suggesting that universities can enhance ChatGPT integration by leveraging these social dynamics (Menon & Shilpa, 2023). By incorporating ChatGPT into academic activities, universities can promote widespread adoption and maximize the educational benefits of this innovative tool (Shahzad et al., 2024b).

Fifth, hedonic motivation positively impacts ChatGPT adoption intention. These results echo the importance of considering pleasure and enjoyment derived from Chat-GPT as a motivational factor influencing users'intentions (Strzelecki, 2023). This hedonic aspect is crucial in shaping individuals'perceptions and preferences regarding adopting innovative technologies like ChatGPT (Ma & Huo, 2023).

Sixth, the investigation highlights that price value negatively impacts ChatGPT adoption intention. Following prior research (Strzelecki, 2023), financial constraints are a well-known characteristic of university students. Many students have limited resources and are cognizant of any extra costs (Raman et al., 2024). Students may weigh the direct expenses of usage or subscription fees for ChatGPT, the indirect costs of data usage, and the possible need for extra tools or resources to incorporate the technology into their academic routines properly (Jo, 2024).

Seventh, moving forward, the investigation unveils that habit positively impacts ChatGPT adoption intention. These results validate the positive relationship between habit and the intention to adopt ChatGPT. The habitual use indicates that indeed, it shapes individuals'inclination towards embracing ChatGPT technology (Strzelecki, 2024). This outcome corroborates previous findings suggesting habituation is a significant factor afecting the adoption of technological innovations (Biloš & Budimir, 2024; Strzelecki, 2023).

Eighth, facilitation conditions negatively impact Chat-GPT adoption intention. The negative impact observed in this study suggests that, despite the availability of these resources, students may perceive them as inadequate, overly complex, or misaligned with their needs (Menon & Shilpa, 2023). This misalignment could lead to frustration and reluctance to adopt ChatGPT, emphasizing the importance of the presence, quality, and relevance of facilitation conditions. Previous studies have often highlighted the facilitation conditions'positive role in technology adoption (Habibi et al., 2023; Jo, 2023a). However, the negative impact found in this study aligns with some advanced research Elshaer et al. (2024), that suggests a more nuanced relationship.

Ninth, the investigation reveals that adoption intention positively impacts the actual use of ChatGPT. The relationship between adoption intention and actual use underscores the relevance of user attitudes and motivations in shaping the practical implementation of ChatGPT. It implies that users who intend to adopt these generative AI technologies are more likely to actively engage with them (Maheshwari, 2023). Consequently, organizations and developers aiming to enhance the adoption and utilization of ChatGPT should focus on cultivating and reinforcing positive attitudes and intentions among potential users (Shahzad et al., 2024b).

Tenth, academic integrity moderates the relationship between adoption intention and actual use of ChatGPT. Students who place high importance on academic honesty are likely to carefully consider the ramifications of utilizing ChatGPT. They might use the technology more sparingly and carefully, making sure it complies with institutional guidelines and moral principles (Cotton et al., 2024). The results of this study have significant ramifications for how colleges handle integrating ChatGPT and other AI techniques to maintain integrity. Establishing a culture of academic integrity inside institutions is crucial in assisting students in ethically using these tools (Bin-Nashwan et al., 2023). To increase knowledge of the possible risks and proper applications of AI tools, educational programs and workshops on digital literacy and ethical usage of generative AI should be implemented (Lai et al., 2024).

## Theoretical Implications

This study enriches the existing literature on technology adoption in education by extending the UTAUT2 framework to the context of generative AI tools like ChatGPT. The study expands the model by incorporating new factors like ChatGPT self-eficacy and academic integrity, enhancing its applicability in understanding technology adoption in higher education. This study identifies habit and facilitation conditions as negative predictors and challenges assumptions that these factors universally promote technology adoption. This insight refines our understanding of how students’ reliance on traditional learning habits or perceived lack of support can hinder new technology adoption. The study introduces academic integrity as a moderating factor, highlighting the ethical considerations involved in adopting AI tools. This adds a new dimension to technology adoption literature, particularly in the educational sector, by showing how students’ ethical awareness impacts their actual use of ChatGPT. Although not explicitly mentioned, the inclusion of factors like self-eficacy and hedonic motivation aligns with SDT, which emphasizes autonomy and intrinsic motivation. The study subtly bridges UTAUT2 with motivational theories, ofering a more holistic view of student behavior regarding AI adoption. This study highlights the diferent dimensions, including PE, EE, SI, CGSE, HM, PV, HAB and FC of ChatGPT adoption, broadening the theoretical landscape by participating in technological acceptance with considerations of responsible usage.

## Practical Implications

For educators and administrators, the findings offer actionable insights to promote responsible ChatGPT use in higher education. Educators should participate in ChatGPT training in the curriculum to enhance students’ self-eficacy and reduce efort expectancy. Administrators can influence ChatGPT to provide personalized feedback and learning assistance, aligning the tool’s use with students’ learning goals. This would improve performance expectancy by showing practical benefits in academic performance. Institutions should promote peer learning environments where students share experiences and strategies for using ChatGPT efectively. Social influence can be harnessed through student communities or AI ambassadors who advocate responsible AI use. Institutions can reward responsible AI use through recognition programs or grading incentives, encouraging students to adopt ChatGPT as a supplementary tool rather than a shortcut to completing assignments. Universities should provide technical support and ensure equal access to AI tools. Facilitation conditions can be improved by ofering dedicated help desks or online resources to address students'technical concerns. Implement systems to monitor ChatGPT use and provide regular feedback to students. This would help educators detect potential misuse while supporting students in refining their use of AI for academic purposes. Additionally, the study underscores the need to cultivate an environment where academic integrity is central, guiding students on ethical AI use while developing policies that responsibly integrate AI tools into coursework. These measures encourage meaningful adoption and help institutions ensure that ChatGPT is used to enhance learning outcomes rather than undermine academic standards.

## Limitations and Areas for Future Research

Despite the robustness of the findings, the study acknowledges certain limitations. First, future research endeavours could delve deeper into exploring nuanced dimensions within each factor influencing ChatGPT adoption. Understanding the intricacies and interplay of these factors could provide a more inclusive understanding of adoption dynamics. Second, as this study focused on diferent parameters that afect ChatGPT using questionnaires, longitudinal user behavior tracking was conducted for future studies. Third, it is essential to acknowledge the limitations of this study as it focuses on specific factors influencing adoption intentions. Future research could explore additional variables like user experience and external influences for a more comprehensive understanding. Fourth, the findings might be context-specific; hence, caution should be exercised while generalizing them across different settings or user groups. Further exploration into the nuanced dimensions of the identified factors could yield deeper insights. Fifth, this study used academic integrity as a moderating variable; for future studies, researchers will consider ethical awareness or ethical concern to build findings. Last, this study used single-country Pakistan university students; for future perspectives, the researchers would use diferent countries or draw a comparison to expand the findings.
